Privacy Policy — YATZY: First Strike

Last updated: draft of 2026-11-21

The short version

You can play the whole game without an account. As a guest, your games, progress and unlocks are kept only on your phone, and the app sends nothing about you to us — except a crash report when the app breaks, which carries no account details and which you can turn off (section 6).

The game shows a video advertisement only when you choose to watch one for a reward (a free life, a boost, extra coins). Those videos come from Google AdMob, which asks for your consent first where the law requires it (section 6).

If you create an account, we store what is needed to give your progress back on another phone, to put your scores on the leaderboards and to run online matches. We do not sell it, and we never give your account details to the advertising service.

1. Who we are

YATZY: First Strike is published by [publisher — to be announced], Romania. For anything about your data, write to [contact email — to be announced].

Under the GDPR we are the data controller for the data described here.

2. If you never sign in

The game is fully playable as a guest. In that case:

  • Your games, statistics, Adventure progress, achievements and cosmetics are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • We keep no account, no profile and no record of you.
  • The one thing the app does online as a guest is read the public leaderboards. Those requests carry nothing about you. Like any connection on the internet, they reach our hosting provider from your IP address; we do not store it or use it.

Why we may do this (legal basis): to provide the account and online features you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep the service secure and working (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. What other players can see

On the leaderboards and in a match, other players can see:

  • your nickname,
  • your avatar, frame and up to three badges you chose to show,
  • your scores, and in a match, your moves.

Everything else is private. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Friends. A friend you accept sees you on their Friends board and can challenge you to a match. Your friend code (the QR code and link on the Friends screen) carries your player number and nickname, so share it with people you want as friends. A friend request can be declined, and a player can be blocked.

The camera is used only while you scan a friend code, after you tap Scan a code and allow it. The picture is read on your phone to find the code; it is not stored or sent anywhere.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules it locally. Nothing is sent to us to make it work. It is off until you switch it on, and switching it off stops it.

We may also send an occasional announcement to everyone (a new region, an event) through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. It is sent to all players at once: we hold no identifier for your phone and cannot address you personally. It arrives only if your phone allows the app's notifications, on its own "Announcements" channel, which you can turn off in your phone's settings.

Supabase processes data for us under a data-processing agreement and may use its own sub-processors for that; it does not use your data for its own purposes.

Google Play and Android may collect crash and diagnostic reports from your phone if you allowed that in your phone's settings. That is between you and Google, under Google's privacy policy; we see only anonymous summaries.

Crash reports

When the app breaks, it sends a crash report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix the fault. A report contains: what went wrong in the code (the error and where it happened), the screen you were on, the app version, your phone's model and Android version, the time, and a random installation identifier that Crashlytics creates to count how many phones a fault affects. If the app was closed by Android itself, the report also carries the reason Android gives. It contains no account details, no email address and nothing you typed.

We do this on the basis of our legitimate interest in a game that works (GDPR article 6(1)(f)). You can turn it off at any time: Settings → Send crash reports. Crashlytics keeps a report for 90 days. Google processes it for us under its data-processing terms; this can happen in the United States, under the safeguards those terms provide.

Reward videos (Google AdMob)

Some rewards can be earned by watching a short video: a free life, a free boost, or extra coins. A video only ever starts because you tapped to watch it; the game shows no other advertising. The videos come from Google AdMob, which, to show and measure them, uses your phone's advertising ID, its approximate location (from the IP address), basic device information and how you interacted with the ad. AdMob is never given your account, nickname or email address.

In the EU, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, in a message from Google, and the ads are personalised only if you agree. You can change your answer at any time: Settings → Privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy as an independent controller for advertising (how Google uses data from apps that use its services).

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Outside the EU, the UK and Switzerland, the app counts which screens are used and for how long, with a random app-instance identifier and an approximate location derived from the IP address, so we can see what to improve. It is never linked to your account and never uses the advertising ID. Inside the EU, the UK and Switzerland it is off unless you consent in the message the app shows on first start (to storing information on your device, measuring content performance and statistics). You can withdraw at any time in Settings → Privacy choices, and counting stops at once. We keep these statistics for 2 months.

No data is sold, rented or shared for marketing beyond what is described above.

7. How we protect it

  • Everything between the app and our servers travels encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access rules on the server let a player read and change only their own account data; the leaderboards expose only what section 4 lists.
  • Passwords are stored only as a hash (section 3).

8. How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Delete your account and it is deleted permanently — the profile, the games, the daily results, the campaign, the unlocks and your games in progress. In a match you played against someone else, your side of the match is deleted and their record shows the opponent as removed.

Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (for example, of sign-ins) for security; they expire on the provider's own schedule.

Data on your own device disappears when you uninstall.

9. Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account (shown while you are signed in). It is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Without the app: follow the steps at this page, or write to [contact email — to be announced] from the address of your account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, give it to you in a portable form, or object to or restrict how we use it. Write to [contact email — to be announced]; we answer within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or to the authority where you live.

11. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If a change affects what we collect or who receives it, we will say so in the app's release notes before the change takes effect.
