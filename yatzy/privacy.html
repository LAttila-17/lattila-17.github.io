Privacy Policy — YATZY: First Strike

Last updated: draft of 2026-09-24

The short version

You can play the whole game without an account. As a guest, your games, progress and unlocks are kept only on your phone, and the app sends nothing about you to us.

If you create an account, we store what is needed to give your progress back on another phone, to put your scores on the leaderboards and to run online matches. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and there are no advertising or analytics services in the app.

1. Who we are

YATZY: First Strike is published by [publisher — to be announced], Romania. For anything about your data, write to [contact email — to be announced].

Under the GDPR we are the data controller for the data described here.

2. If you never sign in

The game is fully playable as a guest. In that case:

  • Your games, statistics, Adventure progress, achievements and cosmetics are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • We keep no account, no profile and no record of you.
  • The one thing the app does online as a guest is read the public leaderboards. Those requests carry nothing about you. Like any connection on the internet, they reach our hosting provider from your IP address; we do not store it or use it.

3. If you create an account

You provide an email address and a password. We then store:

WhatWhy
Email addressso you can sign in and recover your account
Nickname and the number after itso other players can see who set a score or who they are playing
Your chosen avatar, dice set, board, frame, showcased badges and game settingsso your choices follow you to another phone and show next to your name
Finished games: score, categories, game mode, datestatistics, leaderboards and achievements
Daily Challenge resultsthe daily leaderboard and your streak
Adventure progress: stages, stars, vaults, lives, coinsso a new phone restores your campaign
Unlocked cosmetics, avatars and achievementsso what you earned is not lost
A game in progress, with a random identifier of your installationso you can continue on another phone, and so two phones on one account can tell whose copy is newer
Online matches: the moves, the result and who you playedto run the match and show your match history

The installation identifier is created by the game itself. It is not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID, and it means nothing outside this game.

We never see your password. Sign-in is handled by our hosting provider, Supabase, which stores only a one-way cryptographic hash of it.

Why we may do this (legal basis): to provide the account and online features you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep the service secure and working (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. What other players can see

On the leaderboards and in a match, other players can see:

  • your nickname and the number after it,
  • your avatar, frame and up to three badges you chose to show,
  • your scores, and in a match, your moves.

Everything else is private. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules it locally. Nothing is sent to us to make it work. It is off until you switch it on, and switching it off stops it.

6. Who else is involved

Supabase processes data for us under a data-processing agreement and may use its own sub-processors for that; it does not use your data for its own purposes.

Google Play and Android may collect crash and diagnostic reports from your phone if you allowed that in your phone's settings. That is between you and Google, under Google's privacy policy; we see only anonymous summaries.

There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app, and no data is sold, rented or shared for marketing.

7. How we protect it

  • Everything between the app and our servers travels encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access rules on the server let a player read and change only their own account data; the leaderboards expose only what section 4 lists.
  • Passwords are stored only as a hash (section 3).

8. How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Delete your account and it is deleted permanently — the profile, the games, the daily results, the campaign, the unlocks and your games in progress. In a match you played against someone else, your side of the match is deleted and their record shows the opponent as removed.

Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (for example, of sign-ins) for security; they expire on the provider's own schedule.

Data on your own device disappears when you uninstall.

9. Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account (shown while you are signed in). It is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Without the app: follow the steps at this page, or write to [contact email — to be announced] from the address of your account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, give it to you in a portable form, or object to or restrict how we use it. Write to [contact email — to be announced]; we answer within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or to the authority where you live.

11. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If a change affects what we collect or who receives it, we will say so in the app's release notes before the change takes effect.
