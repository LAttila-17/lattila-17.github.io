Delete your YATZY: First Strike account

YATZY: First Strike is published by [publisher — to be announced]. You can delete your account and all data linked to it in either of two ways. You do not need to have the app installed for the second one.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open YATZY: First Strike and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The deletion happens at once and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — by email (without the app)

Write to [contact email — to be announced]
from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject “Delete my YATZY account”.

We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. If you write from a different address, we will ask you to confirm from the address of the account, so that nobody can delete someone else's account.

What is deleted

  • your account and email address;
  • your profile: nickname, avatar, frame, badges and settings;
  • your games, statistics, Daily Challenge results and leaderboard entries;
  • your Adventure progress, unlocks and achievements;
  • your games in progress, and your side of online matches.

Nothing is kept, except short-lived technical logs held by our hosting provider for security, which expire on their own schedule. Data stored on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app, and deleting the account from inside the app removes its copy from that phone as well.
